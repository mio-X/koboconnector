# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026

## 1. Information Collection and Use
This Looker Studio Community Connector (the "Connector") connects your Google Looker Studio environment directly to your KoboToolbox servers via the KoboToolbox API.

The Connector **does not collect, store, or transmit** any of your personal information, authentication credentials, or form data to the developer (Myo Oo) or any third parties. 

All data processing happens securely between your browser, Google's Apps Script servers, and your selected KoboToolbox servers.

## 2. Authentication
Any API keys, Usernames, or Passwords entered into the Looker Studio configuration screen are handled entirely by Google Looker Studio's secure parameter system. They are sent directly from Google's servers to KoboToolbox to authenticate requests. The developer has no access to these credentials.

## 3. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/mio-X/koboconnector/issues
